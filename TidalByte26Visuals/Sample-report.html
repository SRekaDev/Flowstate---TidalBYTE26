How your scores work

The focus estimate starts with camera-facing posture. After 60 consecutive seconds of measured windows with fatigue at 80 or higher, its ceiling falls from 85 at fatigue 80 to 65 at fatigue 100. Missing data or a lower fatigue reading resets the timer. This is an experimental scoring rule. Fatigue compares eye closure with the first two minutes. A possible yawn adds 8 points and a prolonged eye closure adds 4, capped at 16 extra points per measured window. Talking or singing can resemble a yawn. Keyboard silence can include reading or thinking. These experimental scores use separate 0–100 scales.
